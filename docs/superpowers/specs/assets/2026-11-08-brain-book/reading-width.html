Chapter 4 · Architecture

Overview

How an order travels through the platform, and which team owns each part.

The platform is built around one order service. Every channel, the web shop as well as the counter application, hands an order to the gateway, which checks the customer's account before anything is accepted. Solution design, p.12

Why one order service

Before the migration each channel kept its own copy of an order, and the two drifted apart within a day. The team chose a single service in the spring, accepting that the counter application depends on it being up. Decision of 12 March

Who owns what

The gateway and the order service belong to the platform team. Billing stays with the customer's finance department, who publish the interface and announce changes a month ahead.

Support runs on the platform team's rota during office hours. Outside those hours the customer's own operations desk takes the first call and escalates through the agreed channel.

What is still open

Nothing in the sources says who approves a change to the billing interface once the migration project has ended.
