# Sprint dropdown lists the sprints of all my teams

The Sprint dropdown on the Sprint page now lists the sprints of every team of the project the user is a member of, grouped per team (`<optgroup>`) and labelled "Team: Sprint", instead of only the project's default team's sprints (issue #143, 2026-10-27, `agent/issues/143-add-dropdown-to-swap-between-sprints`). A user in two teams with their own sprint folders could not reach the second team's sprints. `AdoClient.allIterations()` reads `myTeams` and fetches each team's `teamsettings/iterations` in parallel, tags each sprint with its team on `SprintIteration.team`, and sorts by start date (no date last), then name, then team. A team whose sprints fail to load is skipped; only when all fail does the call fail. With fewer than two teams, or when the teams cannot be read, the list is the default team's as before, without team labels. `workItems:sprint` takes an optional team name and looks the sprint up and loads its backlog in that team's scope. With nothing picked, the page still shows the default team's current sprint. The choice is not remembered across restarts.

Not verified: against a live Azure DevOps organization (only through the fake server in tests), and on Windows. Not in this change: iteration folders that no team has added to its sprints are still not shown, teams the user is not a member of, the Home page sprints band and the work item picker's sprint scope.
