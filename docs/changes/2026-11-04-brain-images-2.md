# Brain: drawn PDF pages, other picture formats and layout pictures

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-brain-images-2-design.md`. Closes the known gaps of `2026-11-04-brain-images.md`: vector drawings inside PDFs, pictures in EMF/WMF/TIFF/BMP/SVG, pictures on slide layouts and masters, and nested alternate content.

## What changed

- **Canvas.** `@napi-rs/canvas` (prebuilt N-API binaries, no system dependencies, exact version) is a runtime dependency, external to the Vite main bundle like node-pty: `tools/forge/packageFilter.mjs` keeps the package's JS and the target platform's binding package (`canvasBindingPackage`), `forge.config.mts` unpacks it from asar, and the packaged-app smoke check (`src/main/smoke.ts`, run by CI on macOS and Windows) now also loads it, encodes a PNG and renders the drawing fixture PDF through pdf.js from inside the bundle, so a build where the binary cannot load, or where pdf.js cannot reach it, fails CI. `convert/raster.ts` loads it lazily (`loadCanvas`); when it is missing, page rendering and in-process rasterising are skipped with one warning on the source. pdf.js cannot `require` the package itself once bundled (its `createRequire` is built on an `import.meta.url` that Vite's CJS output turns into `{}.url`), so `pdfToMarkdown` sets the globals its renderer expects (`DOMMatrix`, `Path2D`, `ImageData`) and hands `getDocument` a `CanvasFactory` built on the loaded package (`canvasFactoryFor`).
- **PDF pages with drawings** (`convert/pdf.ts`): `hasDrawing` walks the operator list with the current transform (`save`, `restore`, `transform`, form XObjects), counts painted `constructPath` operations (clips, which end in `endPath`, and a path covering 90 % or more of the page, the background, do not count) and sums their bounding boxes in the page's own, unrotated units; a page with at least 8 such paths covering at least 5 % of the page is rendered with pdf.js at a scale that makes the longer side 1600 px and stored as `p<n>.png`, linked `![p.<n> as drawn]` after the page's text; the page's own pictures are then not extracted separately. Rules, underlines and a vector logo fall under the threshold; a diagram or a filled table is above it. At most 20 pages per PDF are rendered, in page order, with a warning for the rest. A render that throws is a warning on the source; the page's text and its own pictures still go through.
- **Other picture formats**: `ImageCollector.addAny` takes any format and sends non-viewable ones through a `rasterize` hook. The app's hook (`createRasterizer`) converts SVG, BMP and ICO in process through the canvas (scaled down to 4000 px on the longer side, so a crafted SVG cannot allocate gigabytes; a BMP or ICO whose header claims more than 25 megapixels is refused before decoding), and EMF, WMF, TIFF and BMP through the platform's own tool with temp files: `Platform.rasterizeCommand` is PowerShell with `System.Drawing` on Windows (the release target) and `sips` on macOS, where EMF and WMF stay unconverted. The hook never throws; a failed conversion is counted in the existing "formats Claude cannot view" warning. The hook reaches the converters through `addSource`'s new `convert` options from the brain handlers.
- **Layout and master pictures** (`convert/pptx.ts`): every embedded picture of each distinct layout and master the slides use (picture shapes, backgrounds, shape fills) is extracted once (`layout-<k>`) into a final `## Slide layouts and master` section, so a template's logo or background is seen once rather than on every slide. On slides, a shape filled with a picture counts as a picture too. The "could not be extracted" marker is now kept per media file, so every reference to such a picture gets it.
- **Nested alternate content**: the resolver now replaces innermost `mc:AlternateContent` blocks first, repeating until none is left.

## Verified

`npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint` green. New tests: a fixture PDF with a drawn diagram (page 1 rendered at 1600 px, page 2 with a single rule not rendered), an edge-case fixture (a page background, a page clip and seven rules not rendered; eight boxes under a 10× transform rendered; eight bars under a 0.1× transform not rendered), a `/Rotate 90` fixture (background page not rendered, drawing page rendered landscape), a BMP header claiming 16000×16000 refused, a render failure kept as a warning, a 22-page fixture for the render cap, a 20000 px SVG scaled to the cap, the marker on every reference to an unconvertible picture, a shape with a picture fill, a master background, the rasterize hook with a fake in PPTX and DOCX, in-process SVG and BMP through the real canvas (read back through the canvas, since its encoder filters rows), the platform fallback through temp files with cleanup and on failure, the Windows and macOS command shapes, layout and master pictures once each, nested alternate content, `keepInPackage` for the canvas packages per target, and the smoke check failing without the canvas. Live, GUI-free: the drawn-diagram PDF and a deck whose only picture is an SVG went through `addSource` with the real rasterizer and the real headless `/capture` on a fresh brain; Claude named every box and line of both diagrams, including the legend of the SVG and the hub role of one component. The converter was also bundled with the project's own Vite config and run on the fixtures from the bundle, which is how the packaged app runs it: a whole-branch review by a separate agent found that pdf.js cannot load the canvas from the bundle (and that a render failure took the whole source down, that clips and backgrounds counted as drawings while a transform was ignored, that a huge SVG allocated gigabytes, that the marker and layout backgrounds were unreliable); all fixed and pinned by the tests above, and the smoke step now renders a PDF in the packaged app on both platforms.

## Known gaps

- EMF and WMF are converted on Windows only; on macOS they are reported, not converted.
- Page rendering costs tokens at capture (a 1600 px page is a few thousand tokens); a long document with a drawing on every page renders 20 pages and warns about the rest.
- A diagram drawn with fewer than 8 paths, or a tiny one, is not rendered; its text labels still reach the page.
- The canvas binding is per platform and architecture; `canvasBindingPackage` knows macOS, Windows and Linux on x64 and arm64.
