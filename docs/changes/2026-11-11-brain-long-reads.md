# Brain: long reads and letters

Asked for: the brain builds itself and the people working with it do not know what is in it; a way to teach them all there is to know and keep them up to date, "reading insightful prose with deep reasoning. Linked sources.", so they can talk shop about the customer's business. This is project 1 of 3: the brain writes for its readers and Shellie shows it. The phone site (project 3, next) and the prioritised reading list with nudges in Shellie (project 2) follow. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-brain-long-reads-design.md`; mockups on the Claude Design canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/LwtD8pqm5RDHjhBWFMKJKp; plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-11-brain-long-reads.md`.

The finished thing, a 1440 × 900 window on sample content: `assets/2026-11-11-brain-long-reads/01-list.png` (the Long reads page), `02-read.png` (a long read), `03-answer-and-gaps.png` (the answer shown, the writer's gaps), `04-out-of-date.png` (pages changed, Update), `05-letter.png` (a letter).

## What changed

- **Long reads and letters are files in the brain.** A long read is `reads/<slug>.md`: one essay per chapter of the book, or on a topic someone asked for, with `title`, `summary`, `chapter` or `topic`, `pages` (every wiki page it rests on) and `written` in its frontmatter. It teaches one subject from start to end around one idea and one worked case, links every claim to its page or source, says what the sources do not say, holds one `Before you read on` question with its answer, and ends with `## What the writer was missing`. A letter is `reads/letters/<yyyy-mm-dd>-<slug>.md`: what you read, what holds now, why it changed, what follows.
- **Skills v10 add `/write`** (`templates.ts`): `chapter <title>`, `topic <text>`, `update <path> since <commit> changed: <pages>`. An update rewrites the long read and writes the letter; a second update on one day adds to that day's letter. Existing brains get it through Settings › Project › Brains › Update skills.
- **Out of date is derived, not written.** After a write job Shellie records in `brain/reads.json`, in the job's commit, the brain's commit and the body hash of every page the long read was written from (`reads.ts`, `stampReads`). A long read is out of date when one of those pages says something else, is gone, or a page joined its chapter. One without a record (written by hand) is never out of date. A long read whose chapter was renamed follows the chapter that has most of its pages among the ones it was written from.
- **The write job** (`jobs.ts`, `brainHandlers.ts`, `readsHooks.ts`): job kind `write` with a `target`, asked for through its own channel `brain:write(brainId, target)`; `brain:run` refuses the kind. It refuses below skills v10, a chapter the book does not have or without pages, and a long read that is gone; asking again for a target that is waiting returns the waiting job. A write job gets 20 minutes. `brain:reads` lists the long reads and letters.
- **Writing by itself** is a switch, off by default: "Write long reads" in What runs by itself and in Settings › Project › Brains, both only on skills v10. On the scheduler's tick a brain with it on queues one background write when nothing else waits and less than half of the day's token cap is used, at most two a day: first a long read that is out of date, else the first chapter without one. A target that failed twice is left for seven days. Topics are only written when asked.
- **Reading in Shellie** (`BrainLongReads.tsx`, `BrainLongRead.tsx`, `longReads.ts`): "Long reads" is the first entry of the Read view's tree, with a dot while something is unread, and the front page says how many chapters have one. The page lists Letters (newest first), By chapter (the long read, or "No long read yet" with Write, its status while it waits or runs, or that the last try failed) and On a topic (with a one-line field to ask for one; a failed topic stays listed with Write). The reader is a serif column (`--font-read`: Sitka Text, Iowan Old Style, Georgia): title, reading time and day, the essay, the question with its answer held back, the gaps in a closed box. Links to pages and sources open in the Read view. An out-of-date long read names the pages that changed, with Update; a letter shows its four labelled sections, what was read struck through; long read and letter link to each other.
- **Read is a mark the reader sets** ("Mark as read" at the end), kept per machine in local storage with the text's hash: a long read brought up to date is unread again. No points, streaks or counters.
- **Activity** names a write job by its target, and Retry of a failed one goes through `brain:write`.
- **Scrub** covers `reads/` and `brain/reads.json`. It does not put a long read out of date: after its commit the record moves to that commit and to the pages as they are, in a second `Brain: scrub` commit, so an update never compares across the scrub (the diff would show the value again). A page a long read was already behind on stays changed.
- **Telemetry** (`docs/telemetry.md`): `brain.screen.action` gains `reads-open`, `read-open` (`kind`), `read-write` (`target`: `chapter` or `topic`), `read-update`, `read-answer`, `read-mark`; `brain.job` gains kind `write`. Never a title, topic, chapter or path, and that holds for refusals too: a refused IPC call's message is sent as telemetry, so the write path's messages name nothing ("The book has no such chapter").
- **Fixes from the review of the whole diff**, each with a test: refusals named the chapter or the file; an unquoted colon in a title made YAML drop the whole frontmatter (now read line by line as a fallback, and the skill quotes the values); the renamed-chapter fallback counted on the long read's pages, so one that also rests on decisions and meetings never matched; a scrub put long reads out of date; a marker or heading quoted in a code block was taken for structure; failures by themselves never expired; an update that renamed its file lost its record; a path with a backslash passed as a long read; "Show the answer" dropped the keyboard focus (the answer takes it now); Settings offered the switch below v10, where it does nothing.

## Known gaps

- **Not run with a real agent.** The prose a model writes from `/write` was not judged here; the owner's trial on their Windows brain will say how the skill text must be tuned. Not run on Windows or on an OpenCode brain either; both are covered by unit tests only (the skill is listed for both agents).
- Read marks are per machine until the phone site (project 3) carries them.
- A letter is written against the long read's previous text, not against what each reader last read.
- A long read on a very large chapter can run past the 20 minutes; the job fails and can be retried.
- A target that failed twice by itself waits a week, also when the cause was passing (no network). The list and Activity say that it failed, and Write by hand is always there.
- After a scrub, the update of a long read that was already behind gets no diff for the pages that had changed; the skill then compares the long read with the pages as they are. Git history still holds the scrubbed value, as before.
- A commit made in the brain outside Shellie does not refresh the open view; leaving the brain and coming back does.
- Reading time counts a `What the writer was missing` heading quoted inside a code block as the end of the essay (the reader itself does not).
- The fallback for frontmatter YAML cannot read takes plain `key: value` lines and `- item` lists only; a value folded over several lines is cut at its first line.
