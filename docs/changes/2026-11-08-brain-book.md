# Brain: the brain as a book

Asked for: read a brain like a book, to learn the big topics of a project as a whole ("the mission statements, who's onboard, general information about the customer's business, architecture"), with a logical table of contents, chapters that read from start to finish, an open page that uses the screen's width, and the original pictures shown inside the pages. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-brain-book-design.md`; the owner chose option C of `assets/2026-11-08-brain-book/reading-width.html` and option 1 of `front-page.html`. No new command and no editor for people: the brain arranges the book, as part of the commands it already has.

The finished thing, sample content, a 1700 px window unless named:
`assets/2026-11-08-brain-book/finished-front-dark.png` (front page), `finished-chapter-light.png` (a chapter with a picture), `finished-chapter-scrolled-dark.png` (the rail following the scroll), `finished-lightbox-dark.png` (a picture opened), `finished-chapter-narrow-dark.png` (1000 px wide, rail under the chapter's head).

## What changed

- **The book file** (`brain/book.json`, `src/main/brain/book.ts`, `brain:book`): an ordered list of chapters, each with a title, a one-line summary, a short introduction and the wiki pages it holds. Skills write it, Shellie reads it tolerantly: a path that is gone, a page listed twice or a back-of-the-book page is dropped, and a file that is not JSON or has no chapters counts as no book. A chapter's summary and introduction say what it covers and why it comes here, no facts, so nothing is kept twice and nothing there goes stale or needs a citation. A chapter with no pages is kept.
- **Skills v8** (`templates.ts`, `TEMPLATES_VERSION` 8; existing brains get it through "Update skills"): the schema describes the book and a new page kind `subject-*` (a subject that fits no other kind, such as the mission or who is on board). Capture and Sync put each page they create, and any page not listed, in the right chapter when a usable book exists; Capture never creates a book. A plain `/sync` reconciles and places pages in a usable book and otherwise leaves the book alone; `/sync book` is the only route that arranges. Digest reviews the book as a whole. The first arrangement writes `brain/book.json` last, in one write, and leaves existing pages and `lastSyncCommit` alone.
- **Automatic first arrangement** (`bookPass.ts`, `scheduler.ts`): once a brain is on skills v8 with pages and no valid book, a scheduler tick (never the launch tick) queues one background `/sync book`. A job that ends without a valid book counts as a failed attempt; attempts are saved per brain and stop after two, and a Sync from Run now still makes the arrangement: `brain:run` for a sync queues the normal sync, then one background `/sync book` when the brain is on v8, has pages, no valid book and no book job pending (a refresh-triggered sync does not). A hand-queued sync no longer drops a waiting book job. The daily token cap pauses it like any brain job.
- **The Read view of a brain with a book** (`BrainRead.tsx`, `BrainFront.tsx`, `BrainTreeView.tsx`, `brainTree.ts`): a front page with the brain's title, the summary of the first page of the first chapter as introduction, Continue reading (the page last read, in localStorage, hidden when nothing was read), and two columns: Contents (number, title, one-line summary, section count; a chapter with no pages shows "Nothing captured on this yet"; a chapter holding a page changed since the last visit gets the blue dot) and the existing graph. They stack on a narrow pane. The tree shows The book (the open chapter expands to its pages, the page being read is highlighted), Back of the book (Decisions, Meetings, Weekly digests, Lessons, Glossary, Sources), and Not placed yet when a page is in no chapter and not at the back.
- **A chapter** (`BrainChapter.tsx`, `book.ts`): the chapter's introduction, then its pages one after another, each under its own title and tags, with previous and next chapter at the end (skipping empty chapters). Any way of opening a page of a chapter (tree, search, graph, a link, Recent) opens the chapter scrolled to that section. The rail's "In this chapter" lists the sections and follows the scroll; Linked from, Sources and Mark verified apply to the section being read. Heading ids get a per-section prefix so two pages with the same heading do not clash.
- **Width and type** (`brain.css`, every page in the Read view, chapters or not): the text starts against the tree and takes all the room left of the rail; `--read-size` grows with the reading column from 14 px at 860 px to 22 px, 18 px at a 1700 px window. Below 820 px the rail folds under the header, as before.
- **Pictures in pages** (`remarkFigures.ts`, `MarkdownView.tsx`, `usePageMedia.tsx`): a link in a wiki page whose target is a picture file becomes a figure after the block holding the link, with the link text as caption and the Lightbox on click, no larger than natural size times a sensible factor. A picture shows once per chapter (or per page outside a chapter), at its first mention. A file that does not load leaves the link alone. Pictures already embedded in sources are unchanged. Picture coverage still counts citations as before.
- **Fixes from the live check** on this branch, each with a test where one applies: the type curve reached only 15.85 px at a 1700 px window (now 18.25); the body sat 16 px right of its titles (one left edge now); a short last section could never become the one being read, so the rail never showed its sources and Mark verified (the end of the scroll now counts as the last section); pictures loading after the text pushed a section just opened out of view (it now stays until the reader moves, or 4 s); title and front-page line height at larger type.
- **Telemetry**: `brain.screen.action` `continue-reading`; everything else counts as `open-page` as before (`docs/telemetry.md`).

## How it works

The book is a file only skills write, read through a tolerant parser against the pages that exist. If the file is missing or invalid, every Read view path falls back to today's tree and landing with the new width and pictures. A chapter is loaded as `brain:read` of its pages, rendered by the same `MarkdownView` with a section prefix. Nothing in the book is duplicated from the pages, so there is nothing to keep in step.

## Known gaps

- A brain with the weekly digest off has its book reviewed only through captures and syncs; nothing re-reads a chapter introduction.
- Not checked on Windows.
- A wiki page keeps its own title and summary twice in the single-page view (the header and the body's `# ` and first paragraph), now larger; the chapter view removes the repeat, the single page was left as it was.
- Long graph labels can clip at the box edge or overlap on the narrower front-page graph (the graph itself is unchanged; zoom and pan were non-goals).
- Continue reading is per machine, in localStorage.
- Not run live: a full `/sync` without a book (only `/sync book`), the scheduler queuing the first arrangement (unit tests), a Digest reviewing the book, an OpenCode brain's arrangement, and reduced motion on the front page's graph.
