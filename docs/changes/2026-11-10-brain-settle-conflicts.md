# Brain: settling conflicts without a terminal

Asked for: an easier way to resolve the brain's conflicts. The owner had never resolved one: "The terminal replaces Shellie, ideally I want a flow to follow to resolve a conflict"; conflicts can be settled from the sources or only after asking someone ("could be both"); a settled fact keeps its history ("History"); no change-request flag ("No"). Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-brain-settle-conflicts-design.md`; mockups on the Claude Design canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/JWaKY49q1TBQijpBRRjygh; plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-10-brain-settle-conflicts.md`.

In the running app, sample content, 1440 × 900: `assets/2026-11-10-brain-settle-conflicts/contradiction.png` (a contradiction, B chosen), `scope-change-answered.png` (a scope change with the answer someone gave), `older-finding.png` (a finding from before skills v9).

## What changed

- **Skills v9** (`templates.ts`; existing brains get them through Update skills): lint writes each contradiction and scope change with a `conflict` (a plain question, claims `a` and `b` with page, quote, source and date, the agent's suggestion, a stable id, `reopens`), reads the decision pages and raises a settled disagreement again only when a newer source disagrees. A new `/settle` skill takes the decision as JSON, updates the pages with every outdated claim kept as superseded (struck through, with when and the decision), writes a `decision-<date>-<topic>.md` page, removes the finding and names the decision page. The schema describes the conflict, superseded facts and decision pages; capture keeps a replaced fact as superseded too. `brain/conflicts.json` is denied to the agents like `pictures-dropped.json`.
- **What the person did** (`src/main/brain/conflicts.ts`): `brain/conflicts.json`, Shellie's only, holds per conflict id either parked (the question asked) or settled (the whole decision, `pending`/`done`/`failed`, the decision page, the error). `BrainFindingSchema` carries lint's `conflict` through untouched, so Shellie's own state writes (after every capture) keep it, and a malformed one never makes `state.json` unreadable; such a finding shows as an older one.
- **The settle job** (`settleHooks.ts`, `jobs.ts`, `brainHandlers.ts`, `main.ts`): a new job kind `settle`. `brain:settle` (skills v9 only) records the decision as pending and queues one job; the job's prompt is `/settle <decision JSON>`; when it ends the record becomes done (with the decision page the skill printed, and an answer source recorded as captured) or failed (decision kept); Retry (`brain:settleRetry`) queues it again; at launch every pending settle without a job is queued again. `brain:park`, `brain:unpark`, `brain:conflicts`; `brain:addText` takes `{ capture: false }` for an answer the settle reads instead. Health leaves out parked and settled conflicts and counts a pending answer as in flight.
- **The Settle view** (`components/brain/settle/`): one conflict at a time (n of m): the question, both claims side by side (quote, page, source, date, Newer, Open page), the agent's read, the choices (A, B, both with how, neither with what is true; for a scope change accept or keep; nothing preselected, the suggestion marked), why, "Settle: …" and Skip for now. I need to ask someone: who can answer (kept on this computer only), a question written from both claims, Copy question, Park. An older finding shows its line with Write what is true and Re-check. Below skills v9 it says "Update skills to settle conflicts here". The list: Needs you, Waiting on someone (who, Copy question, Got an answer: the reply goes in as a source, then the conflict opens with the answer shown and the settle reads it), Settled (choice, status, Decision page, Retry).
- **Entry points**: Add › Activity › Needs you has "Conflicts to settle · n" (or "Conflicts waiting on someone · n", not counted); the Add badge counts open conflicts and leaves out answers waiting for their settle; Health's contradiction and scope-change findings have Settle (Shellie's own findings keep Resolve in terminal); a page in Read that an open conflict involves says "This page disagrees with …" with Settle.
- **Fixes from the final review**, each with a test that failed first: someone's answer is kept with its conflict in `brain/conflicts.json` (`brain:answer` adds the source without a capture and records it; the conflict is open again with the answer however the view was left), and it counts as in flight until its settle is done; `brain/conflicts.json` is committed under the brain's git lock with every park, answer, settle and its result, and a scrub redacts it; a settle job that names no decision page counts as failed ("The settle wrote no decision page") instead of hiding the conflict; failed and pending settles stay listed however old; a pending settle on an archived brain is not run at launch; parking keeps an earlier settle of a reopened conflict.
- **Test runs**: the main test project turns off git's background maintenance. With two more skill files the test brains pass 100 loose objects, a commit then starts an auto maintenance that writes into `.git/objects/pack` while `afterEach` removes the folder: the cause of the `removeSource` ENOTEMPTY flake, which these skills made constant.
- **Telemetry**: `brain.screen.action` `settle-open`, `settle`, `park`, `copy-question`, `answer`, `skip`, `recheck`; `brain.job` kind `settle` (`docs/telemetry.md`).

## Verified

`npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint` green.

In the dev app on a fresh data folder, a brain on skills v9 seeded with a contradiction, a scope change and an older one-line finding, and a fake `claude` first on the login shell's PATH (no tokens spent): the badge counted 3 and Activity offered Settle; the contradiction showed as designed; Settle with B and a why queued `/settle` with the whole decision JSON on stdin; a fake failure was recorded with its error and Retry made it done with the decision page the job printed; parking kept the name out of `brain/conflicts.json`; Got an answer added the source without a capture and the settle that read it recorded it as captured, badge back to 0; a pending settle with no job was run again after a restart. Not run with the real `/settle` skill on a real brain, so the page edits and decision pages a real agent writes were not looked at.

## Known gaps

From the final review, left for later: a reopened conflict settled again shows as open until its settle job runs; opening Settle from Read is counted twice in telemetry; Settle does not say a job waits for the daily cap, Settled rows have no "n pages changed", Skip on the last conflict goes to the previous one; the answer's captured record is written after the settle's commit (the next job commits it as an outside edit); removing a source an unfinished settle cites is not handled.

- Not run with a real agent, on Windows, or on an OpenCode brain.
- Once nothing is open or parked, the Settled list is not reachable from Add; the settles show in Activity as jobs and as decision pages.
- Lint's conflict ids depend on the agent following the id rule; a renamed id makes a parked conflict show as new.
